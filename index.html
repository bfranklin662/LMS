The Ultimate Football Survival Game

Play the Polytechnic FC Last Man Standing Football Game

Pick a team to win, survive the gameweek, and watch the field shrink until one player is left standing. Designed for mobile, built for club teammates, and made to help raise money for Polytechnic FC.

Premier League Top 4 English leagues International tournaments

How it works

Simple rules. Brutal little game

It takes about ten seconds to understand, which is exactly why everyone gets far too invested.

01

Join a game

Create an account, enter the lobby and register for the competition you want to play.

02

Pick a team

Choose one team to win before the deadline. In most games, you can only use a winning team once.

03

Survive or drop

If your team wins, you go through. If they draw, lose, or you miss the deadline, you are out.

Features

Play Last Man Standing with your club teammates

Fast lobby access, current fixtures, live-ish results, player tracking and admin tools without relying on a messy spreadsheet front-end.

🏟️

Games lobby

Run multiple competitions at once and let players jump into the right game from one place.

⚽

Fixtures and results

Players can see upcoming fixtures, recent scores, and their own gameweek status quickly.

🏆

League and cup coverage

Premier League, Championship, League One, League Two, cups and international competitions.

✅

Automatic tracking

Selections, alive/dead status, winners, prize pots and fundraising all stay organised.

Privacy Policy
Last Man Standing

How we use your data

Polytechnic FC uses your details to run the Last Man Standing competition (creating your account, managing entries, contacting you about your picks and competition admin).

What we collect

  • Name, email, phone, team/club connection
  • Your picks and outcomes

Why we collect it

  • To run the competition and contact you about your entry
  • To administer results and verify eligibility

Legal basis

Consent (and legitimate interests for competition administration).

Who we share it with

Only the competition organisers and the services used to host the site/API.

Retention

We keep data for the duration of the competition and a reasonable period after for admin/audit.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion by contacting the organisers.
